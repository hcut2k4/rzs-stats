## Harness corrections
- **Seed-order scorer (2026-11-21):** the first version required the whole predicted seed list to be a bracket candidate, so a mistake elsewhere in the conference failed unrelated decisions. Seed-order accuracy was understated by about 17 points for DSEED and 13 for WSEED. Scoring now uses only the relative order of the tied teams.
- **Label audit:** the full-season sim fixture had DEN and NE swapped for seeds 1 and 2. Fixed against the screenshot.
- **Preseason games:** the inventory counted stage-0 preseason games as regular season until `stageIndex=1` was added.

## Cross-checks
- `plain` reproduces `plain_model.py` on the 36 old conferences (36/36 identical predictions). Against the corrected labels it scores 33/36 (SIMFULL AFC is now a miss because of the corrected DEN/NE order; DM2-2027 NFC and SFL-2026 AFC are the known misses).
- WC3P failures by group shape under `plain5` (train): 3 teams from 3 different divisions with 2 spots: 31/31; same groups with 1 spot: 24/34; 3 teams in the same division: 1/6; 3 teams from 2 divisions: 38/55; 4+ teams: mostly poor but tiny samples.
- Mining (train): the two-team decision list is H2H, division record, common opponents, conference record, SOV in WC2, DSEED and WSEED alike, with 1, 5 and 5 pairs unexplained.

## Open
- WC3P (3+ team wild-card ties) is the largest unexplained category: about 67% on train with `plain5`.
- Next: group-level decision-list induction (keep-best / drop-worst steps scored against the set that qualified), conditioned on whether the group contains same-division teams.
- DIV3P has only 24 train decisions; expect "insufficient evidence" in the final holdout.

### Harness corrections (round 3)
- Ties must be grouped by win percentage, not exact W-L-T (8-7-2 and 9-8 are both .529).
- Madden's playoff-status flags are unreliable and must not define groups: 4THSL 2026 flags HOU as a division winner but seeds it 5th; FML 2027 flags TEN with a bye but seeds it 7th. Division winners are seeds 1-4.

### Unexplained (post-launch)
- Verified: DSM 2027 NFC (DET > WAS > CHI; rule gives DET > CHI > WAS), FIRST 2027 AFC (BAL observed last; rule puts it third).
- Unverified history: 4THSL 2026 AFC South title (all equal through SOV; SOS favours HOU, IND won), BOBL 2026 NFC (CAR got in at 1-3 vs the group), T2H 2026 AFC (CLE got in), LD 2026 NFC division-winner seeding (ATL first).
- These six are 0.15% of decisions; no variant tested fixes any of them without breaking others.
