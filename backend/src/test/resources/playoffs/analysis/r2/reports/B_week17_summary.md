## Scope and definitions

Only its 8 scheduled teams have complete records. Other SIM26 records, full division positions, SOV/SOS, and any order depending on unscheduled opponents are incomplete. The three held-out tie groups use only scheduled teams; strength substitutions and cached division standings remain conditional on incomplete opponent data.

Final overall W-L defines division-title candidates and record tie groups in every main experiment. Substitutions alter only tiebreaker inputs. `rank` uses cutoff overall record then cutoff division tiebreaks to choose the highest-ranked member of each division in WC reduction. `order` replaces every tie ordering with cutoff overall record then cutoff base-procedure ordering, including division-title selection restricted to the final tied leaders. Thus `order` is a strong cached-order hypothesis; it does not let a final inferior-record team become division winner. `div`, `conf`, `h2h`, `common`, `strength` replace division record, conference record, all pair/group H2H and sweep predicates, common-opponent sets plus records, and SOV/SOS respectively. Cached rank/order are complete snapshots from the base model: other substituted fields do not mutate a cached snapshot.

The seven base procedures are the supplied NFL harness; the same harness with its two-club division list in every context; M3; and four M3 ablations (no exception, normal same-division reduction, no inverted H2H anywhere, normal multi-team H2H only). Final NFL and M3 seed lists were checked against the reference implementations for every conference. The harness does not implement coin toss/net TDs and uses its existing fallback; no conclusions depend on identifying Madden internal code. `NFL_div2` changes only the two-club lists, retaining the NFL multi-team procedure.

All 128 subsets of seven stale fields were evaluated, including every single, pair, triple and higher combination, on all seven procedures at each cutoff, for 2,688 scored configurations. Every cutoff-17 row was actually executed: replacing an inverted reduction with an ordinary cached division ranking can change M3 even at the final cutoff, so this control includes a structural change.
